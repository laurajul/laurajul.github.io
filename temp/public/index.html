Call for Papers

Symposium: From Hype to Reality – Artificial Intelligence in the Study of Art and Culture
Date: October 23

Location:
Digital Society Initiative
University of Zurich
Rämistrasse 69
Zurich, Switzerland

Submission Deadline: August 12, 2025

We invite researchers, artists, technologists, and cultural theorists to submit short abstracts for presentations at our 1-day symposium exploring the integration of Artificial Intelligence in the study of art and culture. The symposium aims to go beyond the buzzwords and reflect critically on the practical, theoretical, and methodological challenges of interdisciplinary AI research.

Symposium Focus:
Rather than presenting finalized project outcomes, this event centers on using AI-based research as a starting point for cross-disciplinary reflection. We encourage contributions that address themes such as:

  • Influence of AI on artistic and cultural production
  • Generative AI in the wild and the impact on visual culture
  • Facilitation of artistic exploration
  • Impact of generative AI on perceiving and making of images
  • Artistic responses to and uses of AI technologies
  • Machine vision in curatorial practices

Our goal is to critically engage with AI’s role in contemporary research and cultural practice.

This symposium is part of the project “The Canon of Latent Spaces”, funded by the Swiss National Science Foundation.

Format:
The day will feature a diverse program including keynotes, panel discussions, artist talks, and hands-on workshops. Participants will be selected to represent a range of disciplines, practices, and perspectives.

How to Submit:
Please send a short abstract (max. 300 words) outlining your proposed contribution to [insert submission email or platform] by August 12, 2025. Indicate the intended format (talk, panel contribution, artistic presentation, or workshop) and include a short bio (max. 150 words).

Registration

The participation in the symposium is free and open to everyone. The event will take place on-site in Zurich (Switzerland), and will be live streamed via Zoom.
