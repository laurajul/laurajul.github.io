## Perpetuating Misogyny with Generative AI: How Model Personalization Normalizes Gendered Harm

Our paper analyzing CivitAI — the most active platform for sharing and developing open-source text-to-image models — is now published in Big Data & Society, after previously circulating as a preprint on arXiv.

Drawing on a dataset of more than 40 million user-generated images and over 230,000 models, we find a disproportionate rise in not-safe-for-work content and a significant number of models intended to mimic real individuals without consent. We contextualize these findings through feminist and constructivist perspectives on technology and propose interventions to mitigate downstream harm.
