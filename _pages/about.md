**Hello there! Welcome to my personal page.**



I am Laura Wagner, a PhD student at the University of Zürich. My research is embedded within the project["The Canon of Latent Spaces: How Large AI Models Encode Art and Culture"](https://latentcanon.github.io/) led by Eva Cetinic and generously supported by the [Swiss National Science Foundation](https://data.snf.ch/grants/grant/216104). Currently I investigate the vernacular of open-source text-to-image and video generative AI models with a focus on how they are adopted, personalized and shared in the wild. Particularly of interest is to me,how norms and practices from online communities become encoded into the models themselves, through tagging, retraining, and sharing, and then perpetuate through visual output at scale.
